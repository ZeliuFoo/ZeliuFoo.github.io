I am a Ph.D. student in the School of Computing at the [University of Georgia](https://www.uga.edu/), advised by [Prof. Jian (Jack) Liu](https://cs.uga.edu/directory/people/jian-liu).

My research is on trustworthy AI, with a focus on music and audio. My current interests include:

- **Generative AI provenance and copyright**: data attribution for text-to-music models, training-data auditing via membership inference, and AI-generated music detection.
- **Music information retrieval**: cover song identification across human and AI-generated covers.
- **AI security**: backdoor attacks and defenses.
- **Foundation models for human sensing**: electromyography and American Sign Language generation.
